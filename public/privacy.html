Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

Briefer is a personal automation tool built and operated by a single developer for their own use. It is not a public product, does not accept sign-ups, and is not intended for use by anyone other than its operator.

What it accesses

Briefer reads calendar events from a private ICS feed URL supplied by its operator, and sends email through the Gmail API using an OAuth token the operator granted to their own Google account. It requests only the gmail.send scope, which allows sending email as that account — it cannot read, search, or modify any existing email in the account.

What it does with that data

Each day, the calendar feed is fetched fresh, used to build a plain-text and HTML summary of that day's events, and sent by email to the operator. No calendar data, email content, or account information is stored in a database or persisted anywhere between runs — nothing is logged, retained, sold, or shared with any third party.
